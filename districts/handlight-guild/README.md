# The Handlight Guild

**Origin Name:** Future Hands
**District Type:** Training hall, repair house, community learning center
**Wuerld Role:** First official district of Watertowne Wuerld
**Core Power:** Teaching people how to rebuild systems and themselves

---

## Welcome to the Guild

The Handlight Guild is one of the first buildings on the Watertowne map.

It is not a shiny academy with perfect systems, endless funding, and polished floors.

It is a scrappy training hall where people come to learn, repair, build, protect, and begin again.

The Guild believes broken machines can be repaired.

It believes people are more than their worst chapter.

It believes knowledge should be passed hand to hand, not locked away behind expensive gates and perfect resumes.

But hope still needs security.

Inside the Guild are student records, donated laptops, volunteer accounts, shared learning systems, vendor access, training materials, internal documents, and every messy little process that keeps a community learning center alive.

That means the Guild has doors to lock.

Keys to track.

Risks to name.

Logs to watch.

People to teach.

And forgotten things that need to be found before they become monsters.

---

## Why This District Exists

The Handlight Guild began as **Future Hands**, a cybersecurity and IT project focused on a fictional nonprofit training center.

Inside Watertowne Wuerld, Future Hands is being brought home as the Handlight Guild.

The old project is not being erased.

It is being rebuilt into something deeper.

This district will be used to practice and document real cybersecurity work through the lens of a living fictional organization.

The lore gives it a soul.

The security work gives it teeth.

---

## What the Guild Protects

The Handlight Guild protects more than computers.

It protects the systems people depend on when they are trying to learn, rebuild, and move forward.

Protected assets may include:

* Student records
* Donated laptops
* Staff accounts
* Volunteer accounts
* Shared learning platforms
* Internal documents
* Training materials
* Vendor systems
* Network equipment
* Security logs
* Physical learning spaces
* The trust of the people who walk through the door

After the Collision, these things became more than normal assets.

They became anchors.

A student login can prove access.
A learning record can prove progress.
A donated laptop can become someone’s only doorway into the signal side of the Wuerld.
A forgotten admin account can become a ghost with keys.

The Guild protects these anchors because people are attached to them.

---

## Main Threats

The Guild does not have unlimited money, unlimited staff, or perfect systems.

That makes it real.

The most likely dangers include:

* Phishing messages
* Weak passwords
* Shared accounts
* Lost or stolen laptops
* Over-permissioned users
* Forgotten admin accounts
* Unpatched systems
* Vendor access problems
* Poor data classification
* Incomplete offboarding
* Students or staff clicking unsafe links
* Physical security gaps in learning labs

In Watertowne language, these threats may appear as:

* Phishing Sirens
* Credential Goblins
* Root Wraiths
* Forgotten Doors
* Thin Places
* Unwatched Logs

## Real Cybersecurity Skills Practiced Here

The Handlight Guild is a fictional district, but the work is real.

This district will be used to practice:

* Risk assessment
* Asset inventory
* Data classification
* Access control
* Identity and access management
* Vendor risk management
* Security awareness training
* Incident response planning
* Vulnerability management
* Physical security review
* Low-budget layered defenses
* SIEM and log monitoring concepts
* Policy writing
* Ethical cybersecurity decision-making
